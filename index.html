Cadence

Privacy Policy · Effective date: July 30, 2026

Overview

Cadence is built with your privacy in mind. We do not operate any servers and we do not collect any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Cadence does not operate servers or receive your information. There are no accounts, no passwords, and no email collection. We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking of any kind.

How your data is stored

All information you enter — family member names, activity schedules, reminders, costs, and documents — is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database and your app's private storage. Cadence also offers a Download My Data feature that lets you export everything as a single JSON file, saved wherever you choose. Cadence never transmits this file anywhere; you can use it to move your data to another device.

Subscription

Cadence offers an optional paid subscription managed entirely through Google Play Billing. Cadence does not process payments directly and has no access to your payment information. Subscription transactions are governed by Google's terms and privacy policy.

Device permissions

Cadence requests permissions only when you use the related feature, and all data stays on your device. Notifications are used for activity reminders and are generated locally. Documents are added through Android's file picker and copied into the app's private storage. Calendar sync, if you turn it on, creates a "Cadence" calendar on your device and manages activity events there; nothing is read from your other calendars' events. You can revoke any permission at any time in your device's Settings.

Location search

When you type a location while adding an activity, Cadence sends that text to Google's Places service to show address suggestions. Cadence does not store these searches and never links them to you or your family's data. They are handled under Google's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Cadence is designed for parents and adults managing household schedules. It does not collect data from anyone, including children. Information a parent enters about a child is stored locally on the parent's device.

Data deletion

All app data is stored on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all local data immediately — no deletion request, no waiting period. You can use Download My Data to keep a copy before uninstalling.
